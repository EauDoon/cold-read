cold‑read

A blind pass on finished work before it leaves your hands. One reader per lens, and none of them saw it being made.

Get the skill Read SKILL.md
Contracts, term sheets, fee schedules, external decks and memos.

The author cannot see it.

A context that wrote a draft and then checks it inherits its own frame. It catches typos and misses the wrong premise: a page that breaks a limit the same author set three pages earlier, because to that author both felt true.

A cold reader is any context that never saw the making: a subagent, a fresh session, or another model.

It fires when all three hold.

  1. The work leaves the building. A third party will read it or act on it.
  2. The pass bar can be stated before the draft exists.
  3. Being wrong costs money, reputation or a decision.

Two of three is a smell. One of three is a tax.

LITEThe default. One cold reader, one round, fixes applied.
FULLSomeone signs it, pays against it or acts on it in public. A reader per lens, up to two rounds.
DELTAA bounded revision of work read in the last 14 days. The change, dated claims, the brief and every number are read again.

The rubric comes first.

Three to six criteria, written before the draft. A rubric written after it rationalizes the draft.

Every figure traces to a quoted sourcebinary
No claim is more settled than its sourcequotable
The fee table recomputes from its inputsscripted
"Make it stronger"a mood, not a criterion

A criterion you cannot fail by pointing at text is a mood.

One reader per lens.

Each reader gets the artifact, the rubric and one named lens. Nothing about how the work was made, and no framing of it as good.

  • EvidenceEach claim's certainty against its source's own status.
  • ConsistencyWhere the work contradicts itself.
  • BriefThe request and the work, nothing else. Does it answer the question asked?
  • NamesEvery personal name, classified by where it sits.
  • Hostile readerDoes the argument survive someone who wants it to fail?

A violation a reader cannot quote does not exist. Numbers close only by a script the main thread runs.

One quoted FAIL is a FAIL.

A PASS needs every reader. Passing is what a reader does when it did not look hard enough, so the two directions are not equal.

Three readers are on this artifact. Take one away.

BLOCKED. The Brief reader quoted line 7. Two readers passed it.

Then it stops.

Fix the FAILs and only the FAILs. A fresh reader checks once more, without the list of findings.

Two revision rounds, then a hard stop. The work ships with its failures named, or it does not ship. There is no round three.
